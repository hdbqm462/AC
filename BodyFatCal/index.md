# BodyFatCal Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-01_

BodyFatCal ("the app") calculates body fat percentage, BMI and related metrics.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your data.** The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

## Health data

With your permission, the app reads your sex, date of birth, height, weight and body fat from Apple Health, and can save weight, height, BMI, body fat percentage and lean body mass back to Apple Health. This data is used only on your device to perform calculations. It is never sent off your device, and you can change or revoke access at any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores your preferences (such as unit choice) and your last-entered measurements locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this data.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
